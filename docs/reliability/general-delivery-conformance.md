# General delivery conformance (spec #81)

The supported promise is complete, coherent, approved obligatory inputs across
Retrospective, Prospective, and Ambispective studies, including both consent
families where applicable. A successful replay of one clinical study is not
release reliability evidence for that promise.

Narrative coverage now has a shared finding-authority policy. Token absence,
section-ownership terminology, and uncertain narrative quantities reach the
existing authenticated independent content review. Missing source citations,
invalid envelopes, structured fields, consent safeguards, source identity and
exact-byte publication checks retain their authority. Physical quantity changes
with unambiguous supplied/observed values retain correction requirements;
ambiguous relationships still need independent review. Passing token matching
never establishes clinical equivalence.

PRS accepts advisory narratives using the same disposition as DOCX drafting.
Each accepted PRS narrative retains its cautions; the reviewer receives the
actual XML passage, XML hash and approved facts. Resolutions remain bound to the
final source, passage, artifact and rule identities. Resolved uncertainties stay
in internal history and do not become client warnings. Actual omissions must be
repaired and reviewed again; no one-shot waiver is introduced.

Recovery compares unresolved evidence rather than repeated issue wording. A
strict reduction relative to the best prior evidence state permits continued
repair during drafting and final independent review within the original deadline.
The reviewer reports source paths and unresolved facts; malformed feedback earns
no progress reset. Cosmetic edits, ineffective repairs and
cycling between previously seen defect sets do not earn fresh progress.

## Evidence and its limits

Both ten-case acceptance runners use the same descriptor materialization.
Assertions measure actual study/template families and sparse/rich values rather
than relying on test names. These ten variations still originate from three
related seed studies.

Five additional synthetic clinical inputs cover dental records, neck pain,
sleep diaries, asthma and knee rehabilitation across all five combinations.
Fixed authored Introduction, Objectives, Methodology and ICF procedure passages
exercise ordinary paraphrases, endpoint mentions and spelled quantities. They
are not automatically rewritten to please the validator. Unrelated sections
still use the recorded orchestration adapter. Paired objective omissions must
be confirmed, repaired, reviewed again and delivered as corrected exact bytes.
Additional quantity relationship mutations exercise the independent review
path, and progressively corrected, ineffective and cycling repairs exercise
recovery through the public lifecycle.

These are offline conformance tests with controlled external rendering and
review adapters. They establish workflow behavior and artifact identity, not
real clinical or every-page visual approval. The developer guardrail includes
the delivery replays and rejects implicit as well as explicit named lexical
vetoes; its source fingerprints remain change detection, not semantic proof.

Installation checks, Linux dependencies, independent content and every-page
reviews are unchanged. Fresh, separately authorized Linux Hermes runs with
varied inputs remain necessary before claiming measured production reliability.
The full suite and paid certification are not part of this focused change.

Source routing now has behavioral coverage in `tests/test_source_routing.py`.
All three branches and both applicable consent families exercise the public
approval, drafting, rendering, independent-response validation and delivery path.
Literal authored expectations cover prose and lists, alternate scalar schedule
fields, mixed structured visits,
explicit contacts without a separate inventory, repeated contact labels,
unallocated conditions and qualified background benefits. Model interpretation
remains outside Python. Source quotations, approved visit identity and retained
structured rows constrain a separate display projection; the approved source
bytes never change. Legacy approvals may supply explicit relationships in prose;
ambiguous relationships remain notes, consistent with ADR 0012.
The editable approval surface also preserves mixed record/text lists as typed
tables rather than stringifying record objects into narrative text.

The final content reviewer must audit raw facts against their intended artifact
sections, not accept agreement with a generated projection as proof. Each routing
assessment retains exact source and destination quotations. Missing or malformed
assessments retry verification; clinical omissions retry the responsible drafting
section. A public regression supplies a structurally valid but incomplete mapping,
then verifies correction, changed artifact bytes and a fresh independent review.
Recovery measures the rendered table projection rather than interpretation
rationale or response metadata. These controlled tests do not measure fresh model
judgment or guarantee successful delivery for every possible input.
