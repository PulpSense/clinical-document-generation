# Rebuild ICFs around the client template language

The Advarra Prospective, Advarra Ambispective, and shared Sterling ICF templates will be rebuilt as clean Word-native documents while preserving Client ICF Language and signature structure. Prospective keeps its existing hierarchy and repairs the invalid Legal Rights reference without adding an injury section; Ambispective places its existing-records disclosure inside `WHAT WILL HAPPEN DURING THE STUDY`; one Sterling template uses distinct Prospective and Ambispective Document Section Contracts. All visible headings become real Word styles, internal comments and hidden review content are removed, fields and page furniture are repaired, and study-specific eye, device, procedure, risk, benefit, payment, cost, alternative, and privacy content comes only from the Canonical Approved Source or controlled boilerplate.
