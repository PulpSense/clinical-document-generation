# Control clinical boilerplate as versioned skill content

Clinical boilerplate is version-controlled, approved skill content rather than language invented independently on each run. Generation agents may select, adapt, and connect this Fixed Clinical Boilerplate to approved study facts, but may not invent study-specific claims. Every boilerplate change reruns the complete Branch Acceptance Corpus, trading some drafting flexibility for reproducibility, reviewability, and protection against unsupported clinical or regulatory claims.
